Career & Leadership Path

How You Advance in NSSR

In accordance with Article 3.7 and Chapter 8, NSSR offers a transparent, merit-based progression pipeline from entry-level participation to executive governance.

1

Entry & Engagement

Volunteer or College Rep

Join NSSR through an open call. Support departmental projects, run campus initiatives, and build your track record.

2

Volunteer Observation

1-Month Performance Period

Applicants for Sub-Executive roles complete a 1-month Volunteer observation period within the target department to demonstrate commitment and skill (Article 8.4).

3

Sub-Executive Role

Operational Leadership

Appointed by Executive Committee for a 1-year tenure. Execute key operational duties within HR, IT, Outreach, Event, or Research departments.

4

Executive Committee

Strategic & Board Governance

Elected or appointed as Department Heads, Treasurer, Secretary, Vice President, or President with full EC voting power and policy governance.
