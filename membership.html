Discover your path within NSSR. Whether you are stepping in as a Volunteer, representing your academic institution as a College Representative, or driving governance as a General Member, NSSR offers structured avenues for personal growth, scientific impact, and leadership advancement.

NSSR Participation Tracks

NSSR recognizes distinct tracks of participation. Each track carries specific rights, responsibilities, fee obligations, and advancement opportunities.

Entry Level

Volunteer

The primary entry point into NSSR. Volunteers support specific events, campaigns, projects, and departmental activities while acquiring foundational skills.

Fee Obligation: Exempt (Free)
Appointing Authority: HR Department
GA Voting Rights: Observer Only
  • Support specific campaigns and workshops
  • Gain hands-on project management experience
  • Eligible for 1-month observation for leadership roles
Advancement Potential:

May be appointed as College Representative, Sub-Executive, or Team Lead based on performance.

Institutional Presence

College Representative

Serves as the official NSSR ambassador within partner colleges and universities across Nepal, bridging NSSR with academic institutions.

Fee Obligation: Exempt (1 Academic Year)
Appointing Authority: Outreach Department
Term: 1 Academic Year (Renewable)
  • Official institutional representation & authority
  • Priority access to NSSR fellowships & workshops
  • Certificate of Appreciation upon term completion
Advancement Potential:

Direct consideration for departmental Sub-Executive and Executive leadership positions.

Governance Member

General Member

Formal voting members of NSSR who form the core democratic foundation of the organization and participate in General Assembly decisions.

Fee Obligation: NPR 500 / year
Appointing Authority: Executive Committee
GA Voting Rights: Full Voting Rights
  • Full voting rights in General Assembly (AGM)
  • Eligible to contest Executive positions
  • Access to research groups & publication support
Advancement Potential:

Eligible to contest or be elected to Executive Committee leadership roles.

Career & Leadership Path

How You Advance in NSSR

In accordance with Article 3.7 and Chapter 8, NSSR offers a transparent, merit-based progression pipeline from entry-level participation to executive governance.

1

Entry & Engagement

Volunteer or College Rep

Join NSSR through an open call. Support departmental projects, run campus initiatives, and build your track record.

2

Volunteer Observation

1-Month Performance Period

Applicants for Sub-Executive roles complete a 1-month Volunteer observation period within the target department to demonstrate commitment and skill (Article 8.4).

3

Sub-Executive Role

Operational Leadership

Appointed by Executive Committee for a 1-year tenure. Execute key operational duties within HR, IT, Outreach, Event, or Research departments.

4

Executive Committee

Strategic & Board Governance

Elected or appointed as Department Heads, Treasurer, Secretary, Vice President, or President with full EC voting power and policy governance.
