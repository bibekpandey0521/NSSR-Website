Next-Generation Initiatives

Science in community.

NSSR believes in a model that is by students, for students. Through outreach, we help students, especially those in high school and below, encounter science in an accessible and meaningful way.

Our aim is to promote science education, build curiosity, encourage students to ask questions, and develop the critical thinking they need to understand and explore the world around them.

Our Commitment

Science for every student, everywhere.

NSSR's outreach model is built on sustained presence, not one-off visits. We work with schools over time to build capacity, transfer resources, and create lasting pathways into research for students who would otherwise never encounter them.

How we measure impact

  • Students reached across provinces
  • Schools retaining lab equipment & libraries
  • Mentees continuing into research pathways
  • Teachers reporting improved engagement

Making scientific curiosity accessible, practical, and lasting for students across Nepal.
