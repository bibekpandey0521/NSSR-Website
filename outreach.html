Science in community.

NSSR believes in a model that is by students, for students. Through outreach, we help students, especially those in high school and below, encounter science in an accessible and meaningful way.

Our aim is to promote science education, build curiosity, encourage students to ask questions, and develop the critical thinking they need to understand and explore the world around them.
