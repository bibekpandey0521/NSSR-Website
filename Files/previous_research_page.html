NSSR Summer Research Fellowship

About the Program

The NSSR Summer Research Fellowship (NSRF) is a mentorship-driven program connecting undergraduate and early-career researchers with experienced mentors across a range of scientific disciplines. Selected fellows work closely with their mentors over the fellowship period, developing research skills, exploring open problems, and building a foundation for future academic and professional pursuits.

This year's cohort was selected from a competitive pool of applicants and spans seven research areas, including Number Theory, Astrophysics, Materials Science, Biophysics, Plasma Physics, AI & ML, and Economics. NSSR congratulates all selected fellows and thanks the mentors for their continued commitment to building a research culture among students in Nepal.
